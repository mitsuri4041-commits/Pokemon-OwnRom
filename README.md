Pokémon World

A Massive 3D Pokémon Adventure for Nintendo 3DS

🌍 About the Game

Pokémon World is a large-scale single-player Pokémon adventure inspired by the 3D graphics and gameplay style of Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire.

Explore seven regions, discover hidden locations, collect every Pokémon from Generation 1 to Generation 7, and experience an interconnected Pokémon world.

🎮 Graphics & World

- Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire-inspired 3D graphics.
- Full Nintendo 3DS dual-screen support.
- Detailed towns, houses, caves, forests, oceans and islands.
- Day and night cycles.
- Dynamic weather: sunny, rain, snow and thunderstorms.
- Environmental effects such as ocean waves and lightning.
- Interactive buildings, doors and environments.
- Pokémon visible throughout the overworld.

🗺️ Seven Regions

1. Kanto
2. Johto
3. Hoenn
4. Sinnoh
5. Unova
6. Kalos
7. Alola

- Explore islands and hidden locations.
- Travel between regions by ship and other available methods.
- Discover caves, treasures and secret areas.
- Meet NPCs and complete quests.

🐉 Pokémon Collection

- All Pokémon from Generation 1–7.
- Every Pokémon can be obtained through gameplay.
- Legendary and Mythical Pokémon questlines.
- Shiny Pokémon.
- Six Pokémon can follow the player in the overworld.
- Pokémon storage and habitats.
- Friendship, breeding, evolution and Pokémon care.
- All Pokémon obey regardless of level.
- EXP Share from the beginning of the adventure.

⚔️ Battle System

- ORAS-inspired 3D battle arenas.
- Real-time battle gameplay.
- One active Pokémon at a time in standard battles.
- Individual Pokémon models and animations.
- Unique visual effects for every move.
- Mega Evolution.
- Primal Reversion.
- Z-Moves.
- Dynamax and Gigantamax.
- Terastallization.
- Wild battles, trainer battles, boss battles and tournaments.
- Double Battles and Battle Frontier.

📖 Story & Adventure

- One main rival throughout the adventure.
- Regional Gym Leaders and Champions.
- Villain teams and major story events.
- Legendary Pokémon storylines.
- Ash as the final major boss after the regional champions.
- Large post-game adventure.

📌 Development Philosophy

Pokémon World is intended to be a complete single-player adventure, not just a small demo.

The goal is to build a detailed, living Pokémon world with immersive exploration, expressive Pokémon animations, exciting battles and a large amount of content.

Features will be developed, tested and improved step by step to ensure compatibility with Nintendo 3DS hardware.

Project Status: Early Development
